Frequently asked questions

How do I change the heartbeat speed?

On the main screen, use the minus and plus buttons or drag along the gauge to set any pace from 50 to 140 BPM. You can change the pace while it's playing — SteadyPulse glides smoothly to the new rhythm without cutting a beat short.

What does the Heart Rate Variability toggle do?

When turned on, it adds a gentle, natural drift around your chosen pace so the rhythm "breathes" slightly, rather than staying perfectly mechanical. It's optional — turn it off for a perfectly steady beat.

Does the sound keep playing when my screen is off?

Yes. SteadyPulse continues to play in the background and with the screen locked.

Which headphones or speakers work best?

The heartbeat is a low, soft sound, so it comes through best on anything that seals or fills the ear, or that pushes some air. In-ear and over-ear headphones (wired or Bluetooth) and Bluetooth speakers all work well. We don't recommend open-ear or bone-conduction earphones — the kind that leave your ear canal open or rest on your cheekbone — because they struggle to reproduce the low frequencies and make the heartbeat hard to hear. Your phone's built-in speaker is fine too.

What's free, and what does unlocking do?

You can try SteadyPulse free with a few minutes of playback each day. A single one-time purchase ("Unlock SteadyPulse") removes that limit for unlimited playback. There is no subscription — it's yours to keep.

I already bought it but it's locked on a new device. What do I do?

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase. As long as you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with, your unlock will be restored. If it doesn't, email us and we'll help.

Does SteadyPulse measure my real heartbeat or health?

No. SteadyPulse plays pre-recorded heartbeat sounds. It does not measure, monitor, or record your heart rate or any health data. It is designed for relaxation, focus, and sound, and is not a medical device.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
